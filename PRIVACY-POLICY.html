Sony Dual Remote Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026

Sony Dual Remote is an offline Bluetooth remote-control application for compatible Sony cameras.

Data handling

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It does not include advertising, analytics, accounts, cloud sync, or internet access.

The app stores the Bluetooth address and user-provided display name of assigned cameras only on the device, so the two camera slots can be remembered after the app closes. This information is not sent anywhere.

Permissions

Bluetooth permissions are used only to scan for, pair with, and control nearby compatible cameras. On Android 10 and Android 11, Android requires a location permission for Bluetooth Low Energy scanning. The app does not read, store, or transmit location data.
